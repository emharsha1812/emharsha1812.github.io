This is a complete PyTorch implementation of the Transformer architecture from scratch, inspired by the seminal paper [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) by Vaswani et al. (2017).

The implementation includes the core components of the Transformer, including multi-head attention, positional encoding, and feed-forward networks.

## Implementation

The complete implementation is available in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/emharsha1812/Paper2sCode/tree/main/attention_is_all_you_need).
